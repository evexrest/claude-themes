Pictures beside the chat

Choose a picture or a GIF to sit in the empty space at the side of the chat. One with a see-through background (PNG, GIF or WebP) looks best. It is not cropped, and a GIF keeps moving. Each side can have several: every press of a button below adds the picture once more.

Your own frame

Choose a picture of a frame or border. A PNG with a see-through middle works best.
