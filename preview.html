Weekend in Lisbon Share
I have two days in Lisbon in May. What should I not miss?

Two days is enough for the old centre and one trip out. Start in Alfama early, before the trams fill up, then walk downhill to the river for lunch.

  • Tram 28 from Martim Moniz, first thing in the morning
  • The view from Miradouro da Senhora do Monte at sunset
  • Custard tarts in Belém, eaten warm

For the second day, the train to Sintra takes about forty minutes. A rough plan for the two days:

plan
day 1  Alfama, Baixa, sunset viewpoint
day 2  Sintra by train, Belém on the way back
